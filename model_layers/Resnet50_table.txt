| **Stage**   | **ResNet Name** | **Layers Included**                                                          | **Description**                                        |
| ----------- | --------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Stage 1** | Stem            | `conv1`, `bn1`, `relu`, `maxpool`                                            | Initial feature extraction, reduces spatial resolution |
| **Stage 2** | Conv2_x         | `layer1.0`, `layer1.1`, `layer1.2` (all sub-layers: conv/bn/relu/downsample) | 3 bottleneck blocks, output channels = 256             |
| **Stage 3** | Conv3_x         | `layer2.0`, `layer2.1`, `layer2.2`, `layer2.3`                               | 4 bottleneck blocks, output channels = 512             |
| **Stage 4** | Conv4_x         | `layer3.0` ? `layer3.5`                                                      | 6 bottleneck blocks, output channels = 1024            |
| **Stage 5** | Conv5_x + Head  | `layer4.0`, `layer4.1`, `layer4.2`, `avgpool`, `fc`                          | 3 bottleneck blocks + classification head              |
